# Familiers d’Alambik

Les cinq sculptures sont originales, créées pour Alambik le 7 octobre 2026.
Les identifiants du catalogue et les règles de combat sont conservés.

Les fichiers `.blend` de ce dossier sont les sources éditables des GLB dans
`../../familiers/`. Leur construction reproductible est dans
`tools/blender/familiers_sculptes.py` et ses deux modules, depuis la racine.
La peinture par sommet, les corps fusionnés et les pivots anatomiques sont
conservés dans les fichiers Blender. Aucun modèle d’un autre jeu n’est repris.

`reference_anatomie.png` est une référence de développement visuel originale,
créée avec l’outil imagegen intégré. Elle sert à guider les sculptures ; elle
ne représente pas une capture du jeu. Direction choisie par le propriétaire :
petites créatures fantasy élégantes, avec une anatomie soignée.

## Prompt de la référence

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: visual development sheet for five ORIGINAL small magical animal companions in Alambik, a portrait fantasy alchemical action game.
Primary request: replace crude round toys with elegant little fantasy creatures with convincing anatomy, sculpted heads, expressive but restrained small eyes, flowing shapes. Professional game character design, painterly stylized 3D look with hand painted color gradients. Compact readable low-poly silhouettes that can be built as real mobile game models. Cohesive family yet clearly different anatomies.
Subject: five characters evenly spaced in one clear landscape lineup, each complete, no overlap: 1 a slender violet fox-like ink spirit, four delicate legs, tapered muzzle, swept tall ears, flowing tapered tail; 2 an amber salamander, long graceful low reptile torso, actual four bent limbs with tiny claws, narrow dragon head, long curved tail, subtle dorsal crest; 3 a turquoise aquatic familiar with slender otter/axolotl anatomy, coherent head and body, elegant outward gills and flowing fish tail; 4 a periwinkle swallow-like sylph bird, shaped beak, refined feathers, real broad layered wings and long forked tail; 5 a small moss-jade stone guardian with compact ape anatomy, strongly sculpted brow, integrated glowing turquoise eyes, broad shoulders, differentiated forearms, hands and short sturdy legs. Refined stone facets, not a robot or assemblage of floating cubes.
Style/medium: high quality stylized game art maquette, anatomically thoughtful rather than chibi, original alchemical fantasy, matte painted surface, minimal antique gold details. Eyes integrated in sculpted heads and recessed eye sockets, no giant spherical eyeballs. Small restrained magic detailing only.
Composition: three-quarter view from above around 48 degrees, all front-facing, beautiful silhouettes, neutral slate-blue floor, each framed at equal scale and adequately separated. No text.
Lighting: soft clean warm key, subtle cool ambient, show sculpted planes and elegant contour.
Constraints: no imitation of any existing game, no living inkwells, bottles, books, quills, masks or other object monsters; avoid oversized round balloon heads, bean bodies, eyes pasted on flat oval plates, cheeks, scarves, hats, cluttered jewelry, clumsy blobs, plastic toys. Do not add scenery. Their five tiny projectile concepts can appear below each creature: pale ivory/azure luminous drop, ember petal, slender ice needle, wind crescent, stone mote respectively, translucent trailing light, no dark red enemy bullet borders.
